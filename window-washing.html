Window washing

Streak-free inside and out, with screens and tracks done as part of the job — not charged as an extra.

Get a Free Quote 647-706-5123

More than wiping the glass

A window cleaned properly stays clean far longer, because most of what makes glass look dirty again quickly is sitting in the frame, not on the pane.

Glass, inside and out

Both faces of every accessible window, finished with a squeegee rather than a cloth — which is what actually eliminates streaking.

Screens

Removed, washed and refitted. Screens hold a surprising amount of dust, and putting dirty screens back over clean glass undoes half the work.

Tracks and sills

Vacuumed and wiped out. This is the part most people skip and the reason windows get gritty again within a fortnight.

Frames wiped down

The visible frame edges cleaned so the whole opening looks finished rather than just the glass.

Hard water spotting

Sprinkler overspray and mineral deposits need specific treatment, not more scrubbing. Left long enough these etch permanently into the glass — worth dealing with before that point. We'll tell you honestly if a pane is already past saving.

Twice a year is the sweet spot. Once in spring after the winter grit and pollen, once in autumn before the weather turns. That's enough to stop mineral deposits ever getting a foothold.

Common questions

Do I need to be home?
Only if you want the insides done — we'll need access to each room. For exterior-only work you don't need to be there at all, as long as gates are unlocked and we can reach an outside tap.
What if it rains right after?
Clean glass doesn't spot the way dirty glass does — rain on a properly cleaned window mostly runs straight off. It's dirty glass that leaves marks when it dries.
Can you remove hard water stains?
Usually, if they're caught early. Mineral deposits from sprinklers eventually etch into the glass itself, and at that stage no amount of cleaning brings it back — the pane needs replacing. We'll always tell you which one you're dealing with rather than charging you to scrub something that won't improve.
Do you do commercial storefronts?
Yes, including regular scheduled visits. Most commercial clients book weekly or monthly so the frontage always looks presentable.
